I'd be happy to help you create a 4-day Mumbai visiting plan. Here's a suggested itinerary:

Day 1: Gateway of India and Marine Drive

* Morning: Start your day at the iconic Gateway of India, a historic monument that marks the entrance to the Port of Mumbai.
* Afternoon: Take a stroll along Marine Drive, also known as the "Queen's Necklace," which offers breathtaking views of the Arabian Sea and the city skyline.
* Evening: Head to Colaba Causeway for dinner and shopping. This popular street is lined with street food stalls, cafes, and boutiques selling everything from souvenirs to clothing.

Day 2: Mumbai's Historical Landmarks

* Morning: Visit the Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus (CST) railway station, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the busiest railway stations in the world.
* Afternoon: Head to the Haji Ali Dargah, a beautiful mosque located on a small island off the coast of Mumbai. Take a ferry to the island and enjoy the stunning views of the city.
* Evening: Visit the Mani Bhavan Museum and Library, which is dedicated to the life and works of Mahatma Gandhi.

Day 3: Markets and Museums

* Morning: Explore the bustling streets of Chor Bazaar, one of the oldest and largest markets in Mumbai. You can find everything from antiques to street food.
* Afternoon: Visit the National Gallery of Modern Art, which features a collection of modern and contemporary Indian art.
* Evening: Head to the Haji Ali Park for a peaceful evening walk and enjoy the sunset.

Day 4: Beaches and Waterfront

* Morning: Spend the day at Juhu Beach, a popular spot for locals and tourists alike. Enjoy the sun, sand, and surf, and try some of the local street food.
* Afternoon: Take a stroll along the Worli Sea Link, a modern highway that connects Mumbai to the suburbs. You can enjoy the views of the city and the sea.
* Evening: Head back to your hotel or explore the nearby area for dinner and shopping.

This is just one possible itinerary, and you can customize it to suit your interests and preferences. Additionally, you may want to consider purchasing a SIM card or portable Wi-Fi hotspot to stay connected during your trip.
